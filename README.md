# Lab5_Completed - Oakville by the Lake

**Lab 5 · Design with Sound & Vision**

An interactive soundscape and illustrated visualscape inspired by Oakville’s waterfront. The project follows a quiet moment by the lake: waves and birds at the shoreline, footsteps passing by, and a return to stillness.

## Soundscape

The 72-second soundscape uses original synthesized audio to evoke the waterfront. It has three chapters: **Shoreline** (0:00), **Footsteps** (0:24), and **Quiet water** (0:48). The audio was created in JavaScript and is available as a WAV download from the page.

## Visualscape

The waterfront illustration responds to the HTML5 audio player. Ripples move during playback and stop when the audio is paused. The sky and water change colour as the soundscape moves through its chapters. Chapter buttons let listeners jump to a specific moment and see the scene update.

## How it works

The finished page is self-contained in `index.html`. It uses HTML5 audio controls, an inline SVG illustration, CSS animations, and JavaScript media events to connect sound and visuals. The page respects the visitor’s reduced-motion preference.

The audio and illustration are original. No external recordings, images, or libraries are used.
